Your numbers

BMI uses weight and height only. Body fat % is an estimate from BMI, age and sex (Deurenberg formula) and can be off by several points, especially for very muscular people. A tape measure or skinfold test is more accurate.

Your plan

Warm up 5 minutes first. Rest 60–90 seconds between sets. When you can finish all reps easily, add a little weight or one more rep. Stop if anything hurts, and check with a doctor before starting if you have a health condition.
